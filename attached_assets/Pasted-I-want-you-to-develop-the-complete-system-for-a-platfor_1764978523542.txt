Campaign Flow

There are two types:

Campaigns with overall goal

All orders placed during the campaign are only validated if the sum of all orders reaches the defined target.
If the goal is not reached, all orders are disregarded.

Campaigns without goal

Each order is validated individually, as long as the store meets the minimum order value or other rules.

Campaigns are important and must appear prominently on the home screen.

Cashback

For every purchase, the store receives a cashback percentage defined by the supplier.
This percentage may vary by state.

Cashback is calculated over the total amount excluding taxes.

If there is no automatic SEFAZ integration, the store may upload:
– the invoice DANFE
– a picture of the budget (when there is no invoice)

The supplier must send a monthly billing report for verification.

The retailer must have an area to request withdrawal, providing:
– PIX key
– required data

The amount can only be withdrawn once confirmed.
